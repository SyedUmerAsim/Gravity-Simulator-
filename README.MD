# **Gravity Simulator**
a simplistic, text-based gravity simulator, which demonstrates the pull of gravity on different planets(all 8 planets) and also the Sun.

## **Design**
I wanted to make it look as similar to a chalkboard as possbile, hence the green background, and then using shades of white for text and shapes to make sure nothing goes "out of place".

## **How it works**
You start off on Location: Earth, Gravity: 9.8 m/s², use the 'Drop from Top' button(dotted circle) to make the little box fall from top to bottom. Then, if you wish to learn more, click 'show planets?' and from the list infront of you, choose any planet you'd like. Then you'd be on Location: (selected Planet), Gravity: (said Gravity), now again use the 'Drop from Top' button (dotted circle) to again make the box fall from top to bottom(and again, and again, as many times as you wish!).
Check the "Show Height Measurements" checkbox to see a NOT TO SCALE scale of height, 10m --> 5m --> 0m on the side of your big dashed box.

*NOTE*
all planet gravities are as provided by a google search, except saturn. I found 2 values, at poles: 12.1 m/s², at equator about        8.96 m/s², so I took an average, which is equivilant to 10.53 m/s²
